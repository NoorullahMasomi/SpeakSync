# Changes

Each `## <version>` part is the text people see on the GitHub release page.

## 1.0.0
First version.

- Any video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI and more.
- Script: paste text (a blank line = a new part) or open a .txt / .srt file. Times like `[0:01 - 0:03]` are read.
- Mark: press **M** where you start speaking and **M** again where you stop. Drag the edges on the time line.
- Record: red / yellow / green light, the text lights up when it is your turn, a bar shows the time left,
  stops by itself when you are quiet, one recording per part (redo only one part, old tries are kept).
- Two timing modes: **Voice fits the video** and **Video waits for me** (the picture waits while you finish).
- Make video: original sound volume, quieter under your voice, your voice volume, subtitles (.srt) included.
- English and Dari (right-to-left) screens. More languages can be added in `speaksync/web/i18n/`.
- "New version" message when an update is published.
