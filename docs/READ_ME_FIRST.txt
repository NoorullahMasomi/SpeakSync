SpeakSync - your voice on any video
Free and open source. Made by Noorullah Masomi.
https://github.com/Noorullahmasomi/SpeakSync
LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/noorullah-masomi-34226a29b/

START
  Double-click SpeakSync.exe. The first time, click "Allow" when it asks for the microphone.
  (Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new: click "More info", then "Run anyway".)

HOW IT WORKS
  1. New project: choose your video and paste your script. A blank line starts a new part.
     You can put the time in front of a part:   [0:01 - 0:03] Hello and welcome!
  2. Mark: play the video. Press M where you start speaking and M again where you stop.
  3. Record: press Space.  RED = wait,  YELLOW 3-2-1 = get ready,  GREEN = read the bright text.
     It stops by itself when you are quiet and the next part comes.  R = record again,  L = listen.
  4. Make video: choose where to save. You also get subtitles (.srt).

YOUR FILES
  Each project is one folder (in Documents\SpeakSync unless you choose another place):
    project.speaksync   the project (you can double-click it)
    takes\              your recordings, one file per part (older tries are kept in takes\old)
    export\             finished videos
  Nothing is uploaded. Your videos and your voice stay on your computer.
  The only internet use: once a day SpeakSync asks GitHub whether a new version exists.

NEW VERSIONS
  When a new version is ready, a green "New version" box shows at the top. Click "Get it".

PROBLEMS?
