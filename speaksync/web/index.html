SpeakSync

Your voice on any video

Mark where you speak, read your script when the light is green, and get a finished video.

Free for everyoneOpen sourceStays on your computer
1Add video and script

Choose any video. Paste your text: a blank line starts a new part.

2Mark where you speak

Play the video and press M where you start talking and M again where you stop.

3Read and record

Red = wait, yellow = get ready, green = speak. Then press Make video.
