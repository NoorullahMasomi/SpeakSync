  The full license: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html
  The source code of FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#get-sources
  ffmpeg.exe is a separate program that SpeakSync starts; it is not changed by SpeakSync.
